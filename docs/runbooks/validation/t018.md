# Compound Copter routes (T-018)

## Fixed protocol

T-019 establishes the stationary, straight and single-turn baseline and its
five-second prediction limits. Reuse its Copter 4.7.0 `+`, home
37.7749,-122.4194,10 m MSL, system/component 1:1, real-time simulation and
external pymavlink 2.4.49 driver. No simulated position or attitude is injected.

At 20 m relative altitude and requested 5 m/s, visit these finite Guided targets:

- Figure-eight: north = 40 sin(t), east = 20 sin(2t), t = i pi/8,
  i = 1..32. Two circuits, with crossings at i = 8,16,24,32 and opposite
  signed turns in each lobe. Nominal envelope is ±40 m north and ±20 m east.
- Traveling snake: north = 15 i, east = 20 sin(pi i/2), i = 1..10.
  Five alternating lateral extrema while progressing 150 m north. Nominal
  envelope is 0..150 m north, ±20 m east. Stop at (150,0), then land.

These are waypoint approximations, including acceleration and corner slowing;
they do not reproduce analytic motion or guarantee constant speed. Each target
must be observed within 3 m horizontally and 2 m relative altitude in 90 s.
All pattern positions must remain within 2 m of commanded altitude. Each run
holds for 10 s after the route, then must LAND/disarm in 90 s. The driver drains
three additional seconds to forward final state. Recreate the simulator before
both patterns and a second figure-eight run with `--interrupt`. The latter
withholds backend position for eight seconds between circuits while TCP truth
continues. Require stale trajectory by 5.25 s after last position and recovery
within 2 s after restoring 5 Hz, as in T-019.

Prediction acceptance: five-second rendered endpoint versus later independent
TCP position, nearest host-time sample within 150 ms, pattern p95 <=30 m,
minimum 20 comparisons. This broad maneuver bound is not steady-flight
accuracy: report the T-019 steady 2.75 m p95 separately. Match UDP/backend boot
timestamps: >100 samples, horizontal differences <=0.02 m, both altitude
fields <=0.002 m and horizontal velocities <=0.002 m/s. Report DataFlash
POS/ATT nearest-boot-time comparisons independently. Track must reach at least
100 points and never exceed 500. Compare the rendered heading with independently received VFR_HUD heading
within 150 ms (>=20 samples, p95 difference <=10° during the pattern);
inspect heading versus ground course and bank reversals; the paths, controls and rendered prediction remain distinct evidence.

## Reproduce

Follow the T-019 setup and recording procedure with a fresh run directory:

```sh
GCS_RECORDING_ENABLED=true docker compose -f docker-compose.yml -f scripts/validation/t019.compose.yml up -d --force-recreate ardupilot-sitl-copter-1
curl -X POST http://localhost:8080/api/recordings/start
```

Start the driver in one terminal:

```sh
/private/tmp/yalb-t019-venv/bin/python scripts/sitl_motion.py --scenario figure-eight --output docs/temp/evidence/T-018/RUN/flight
```

Immediately start the observer in a second terminal:

```sh
MOTION_CAMERA_SMOKE=1 node scripts/validation/observe-motion.mjs docs/temp/evidence/T-018/RUN/browser 360
```

After both processes finish (retain the ID returned by recording start):

```sh
curl -X POST http://localhost:8080/api/recordings/stop
docker compose cp ardupilot-sitl-copter-1:/home/sitl/logs/00000001.BIN docs/temp/evidence/T-018/RUN/autopilot.BIN
/private/tmp/yalb-t019-venv/bin/python scripts/validation/analyze-motion.py docs/temp/evidence/T-018/RUN RECORDING_ID
/private/tmp/yalb-t019-venv/bin/python scripts/validation/compare-motion-log.py docs/temp/evidence/T-018/RUN
MOTION_REPLAY_SPEED=4 node scripts/validation/observe-motion.mjs docs/temp/evidence/T-018/RUN/replay 100 'http://127.0.0.1:3000/?source=replay&recording=RECORDING_ID'
python3 -m unittest discover -s scripts -p 'test_sitl_motion.py'
./scripts/kanban check
```

Repeat using `--scenario snake`, then `--scenario figure-eight --interrupt`.
Start browser and driver concurrently; wait for both before analysis. Increase
browser duration if startup or route flight extends beyond its capture window.
Camera smoke performs an actual mouse drag after 90 s and clicks Follow after
100 s; after 2 s of drag settling, require <=0.1 m center drift while manual,
then <=1 m p95 center-to-track-end distance with Follow pressed (>=10 samples); inspect the captured center and pressed state to establish manual
persistence and resumed following. Replay at 4× checks route sequence and
rendering, not live wall-time freshness. Keep artifacts under docs/temp/evidence.
Restore the ordinary Compose simulator link afterward; retain recording volume.

Runs on 2026-09-16 used Copter 4.7.0 release, custom build `1511f271`,
model `+`, real-time speedup 1, Chrome headless 1600×1000 and the local
Compose backend/UI. The T-019 stationary/straight/single-turn acceptance is
reused; this task adds compound routes and repeats interruption/recovery.

All position comparisons differ by less than 3 nanometres (floating-point
serialization); MSL/relative altitude and north/east velocity differences are
zero. The UI heading check compares asynchronous browser and TCP observations,
not wire-identical updates; yaw changes quickly at corners. Heading is distinct
from the course calculated from north/east velocity. Browser screenshots show
both figure-eight lobes/crossing and successive traveling snake reversals.

The snake camera smoke recorded 36 settled manual samples with zero center
drift and 275 resumed samples with zero p95 center-to-track-end error. Manual
mode was unpressed for Follow throughout the settled interval; Follow was
pressed again after the real button click. Analysis is limited to the driver’s capture window; resumed samples include
the remaining route, settling, landing and terminal telemetry.

Independent DataFlash checks: figure-eight POS 944 samples, <=9.98 ms alignment,
max horizontal/MSL/relative differences 0.057/0.030/0.025 m; ATT 1,889 samples,
<=17.98 ms alignment, max roll/pitch/yaw differences 0.75/0.74/1.26°.
Snake POS 639 samples, <=37.48 ms alignment, max differences
0.200/0.100/0.097 m; ATT 1,279 samples, <=47.46 ms alignment, max differences
1.96/2.29/3.48°. These support units and coordinate-frame interpretation under
asynchronous sampling, not physical sensor accuracy.

Recording 9 replayed at 4× to its 3:44 event endpoint, with 412 browser samples,
zero exceptions, the figure-eight route, 500-point track and final disarmed
state (the UI offers ARM). Playback duration is the event span, excluding the
recording's initial telemetry-free startup. Replay validates rendering/sequence;
wall-time deadlines are measured live.

The interrupted figure-eight (recording 11) reached all 32 waypoints with a
maximum 2.997 m horizontal error. Pattern duration including the pause was
141.05 s; LAND/disarm took 30.99 s. Relative-altitude error stayed <=0.014 m.
Its 596 prediction comparisons gave 20.68 m p95 with <=102.2 ms alignment;
the interruption/recovery hold is excluded from maneuver statistics rather
than diluting turn errors with stationary observations. Rendered heading
comparison gave 8° p95; both roll signs and both lobes recurred.

There were 1,170 exact UDP/backend matches, with the same negligible position
and zero altitude/velocity differences; receipt skew <=7.43 ms. The actual
backend position gap was 8.203 s. The trajectory source became absent 5.047 s
after the last position and returned 0.029 s after stream restoration, passing
the fixed 5.25 s / 2 s deadlines. During the gap the UI showed absent latitude,
longitude and altitude; heading and attitude stayed live. This fault is an
external rate request to SITL on the backend link, not aircraft motion input
or replacement telemetry. The independent TCP position stream continued.

The repeated camera smoke again passed: 36 settled manual samples, zero drift;
661 resumed samples, zero p95 follow error. Track stayed bounded at 500;
browser exceptions were zero. Independent DataFlash POS 1,035 samples had
<=21.36 ms alignment and max horizontal/MSL/relative differences
0.105/0.050/0.052 m. ATT 2,068 samples had <=13.36 ms alignment and max
roll/pitch/yaw differences 0.54/0.54/0.96°.

Recording 11 replayed to its 4:02 endpoint at 4×, with 413 browser samples,
zero exceptions and the final disarmed state. The recorded position fault
removed/restored the trajectory (0.615 s of wall-time absence at 4×).
Accelerated replay also showed brief startup trajectory transitions of about
0.10–0.21 s; it is not claimed to reproduce live rendering cadence. Only the
live capture establishes freshness deadlines.

All nine runner/geometry tests, Python compilation, browser-script syntax,
three analyzer runs, `git diff --check` and task-board validation passed.
The ordinary direct Compose simulator feed was restored. After acceptance,
the operator requested cleanup: all T-018 temporary captures and backend
recordings 9, 10 and 11 were deleted on 2026-09-16. Recording IDs above
identify the historical runs; repeat the procedure to generate fresh evidence.
The written results and reusable validation scripts remain.

The observed routes are polygonal Guided waypoint flights with slowing at
corners, not smooth analytic figure-eights or sinusoidal S-turns. The measured
maneuver errors pass the broad 30 m bound but do not establish accurate future
motion through command changes. The earlier T-019 steady interval remains
2.75 m p95 under its narrower conditions; compound results do not extend that
claim. No Plane, wind, hardware or general long-flight equivalence is claimed.
